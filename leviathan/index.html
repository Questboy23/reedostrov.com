Project 01 · macOS · v3.0

Leviathan

It stops viruses on your Mac, keeps AI agents away from your secrets, and turns on a VPN on networks you don’t trust.

Download for Mac ↓View the code

Double-click the zip in Downloads, then double-click Install Leviathan.command. The first time, macOS blocks it because it is not from the App Store: open System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway, then double-click it again. It needs Homebrew. The shield appears in your menu bar.

What it does

Leviathan does three jobs. It stops viruses, including the ones hidden in cracked and pirated apps. It keeps AI agents like Claude Code and Codex away from your passwords, keys and files. And it turns on a free VPN by itself when you join Wi-Fi you don’t trust.

It runs quietly in the background. After a one minute setup there is nothing to click and nothing to keep up to date. When it does step in, it tells you in plain words what it found and why, and it keeps a record so you can reverse the action later.

How it works

1. Untrusted apps open in a Safe Box

When you download an app that Apple never checked, which covers most cracked and pirated apps, Leviathan moves it into a box and leaves a launcher with the same name and icon. You open it as usual. Inside the box it cannot see your files, browser data, passwords, keychain or crypto wallets, cannot show a fake password box, and cannot set itself to start at login. One command takes it out again.

2. Harmful downloads get locked away

New files in Downloads and new apps are checked with the same malware rules Apple ships in macOS, plus Apple’s list of developers it has banned. A match goes to the Trash with its permission to run removed, so it cannot open even from there. One command puts it back.

3. Bad websites never load

A standard macOS profile sends every website lookup, encrypted, to Quad9 (9.9.9.9). Known malware and phishing sites fail to load, in every app. Legal sites stay open.

4. Pasted scam commands and fake password boxes get stopped

A popular scam asks you to paste a line into Terminal to “fix” something. Leviathan stops those commands once and explains the risk. It also closes pop-ups that ask for your Mac password, and switches off malware that hides in your startup items.

5. AI agents work in a box

Start an agent with leviathan agents run -- claude and it can work on your project and nothing else: no SSH keys, browser data, keychains, wallets or Documents. With “Watch AI agents” on, fake secret files sit on your Mac; one carries a planted instruction, and an agent that obeys it is paused and named. Every event can be exported for AI safety research, with your home folder hidden.

6. A VPN on Wi-Fi you don’t trust

Leviathan drives Cloudflare WARP, which is free. At home the VPN stays off. At cafes, hotels, airports and school it turns on by itself, and the menu shows whether your traffic really goes through it.

7. A shield in your menu bar

White while Leviathan waits, green while a scan runs, orange when it has a warning you have not seen. Copying text never triggers anything.

How to install

  1. 1
    DownloadClick Download for Mac above, then double-click the zip in your Downloads folder to unzip it.
  2. 2
    Open TerminalPress Cmd and Space, type Terminal, press Return.
  3. 3
    Run the installerType bash with a space, drag install.sh from the unzipped folder into the window, and press Return. No password needed.
  4. 4
    Turn on the website filterSystem Settings opens at the end. Double-click Leviathan website filter, click Install, and enter your Mac password.

Or paste this single line into Terminal:

cd ~ && curl -fsSL https://github.com/Questboy23/leviathan/archive/refs/tags/v3.0.1.tar.gz | tar xz && bash leviathan-3.0.1/install.sh
