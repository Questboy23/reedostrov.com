Activity Monitored

Keeps your Mac quick when too much is running at once.

Download for Mac ↓View the code

What it does

When many programs compete for your Mac's processor, everything slows down, including the window you are using.

Activity Monitored notices when that happens. It lets busy background programs wait their turn, so the app in front of you stays fast. A minute or two later it puts them back to normal.

Why it helps

  • 01
    Your current app stays smoothTyping, scrolling and video keep up even while heavy work runs behind them.
  • 02
    Heavy jobs can finish soonerIn one overload test, a 10 program job finished in 24 seconds instead of 39.
  • 03
    Nothing is ever closedPrograms are only slowed for a moment. They keep running and keep their work.
  • 04
    Undoes itselfEvery change has a timer that reverses it automatically, even if Activity Monitored stops.
  • 05
    Free, with no accountNo subscription, no sign up, and no data leaves your Mac.

How to install

  1. 1
    DownloadClick Download for Mac above. Your Mac unzips it into Downloads.
  2. 2
    Open the installerIn the unzipped folder, right click Install Activity Monitored.command and choose Open.
  3. 3
    DoneIt starts now and every time you log in. If your Mac offers to install developer tools, click Install, then repeat step 2.

Or paste this single line into Terminal:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Questboy23/activity-monitored/main/install.sh | bash
